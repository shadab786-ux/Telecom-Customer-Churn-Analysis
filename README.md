<<h1> Telecom Customer Churn Analysis<h1>
<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>
<p>Customer churn is one of the most critical metrics for telecommunications companies. <br>This project performs an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on telecom customer dataset to uncover key drivers behind customer churn, identify high-risk segments, and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention strategies.</p>
<h2>🎯 Key Objectives</h2>
<p>1)Conduct data cleaning and preprocessing (handling missing values, data type conversions).
  <br>
2)Analyze overall customer churn distribution and establish baseline metrics.
   <br>
3)Examine the relationship between tenure, charges, and customer churn.
   <br>
4)Evaluate the impact of contract types, payment methods, and internet services on customer retention.
   <br>
5)Provide data-driven retention strategies to reduce churn among vulnerable customer groups.</p>
<h2>📊 Key Insights & Dataset Highlights</h2>
<h4>Dataset Size:</h4> 7,032 customer records (after cleaning missing TotalCharges).

<h4>Overall Churn Rate:</h4> 26.58% (1,869 churned vs. 5,163 retained customers).

<h4>Contract Risk:</h4>

Month-to-month contract holders have the highest churn rate at 42.71%.

Two-year contract holders exhibit the lowest churn rate at just 2.85%.

<h4>Internet & Add-On Services:</h4>

Fiber optic subscribers experience higher churn (41.89%) compared to DSL users (19.00%).

Lack of Tech Support and Online Security strongly correlates with higher churn rates.

<h4>Payment Methods:</h4>

Customers using Electronic check payees churn at 45.29%, significantly higher than automated bank transfer or credit card users (~15–16%).

<h4>Financial Profile:</h4>

Average tenure for churned customers is 18.0 months vs. 37.7 months for retained customers.

Churned customers pay a higher average monthly charge ($74.44) compared to non-churned customers ($61.31).
